## Small ads (Facebook/Instagram, later)

- Audience: 25 km around Laval, ages 25–55 (the people who collected as kids in 1999–2005).
- Budget to test: C$100–300 over 2 weeks. Measure cost per submission and per collection bought.
- Headline: « Vos vieilles cartes Pokémon valent peut-être plus que vous pensez »
- Text: « Envoyez des photos, recevez une offre en argent comptant en 24 h. On se déplace à Laval et Montréal. »

## Tips

- Answer fast: the first fair offer usually wins.
- Meet at a police station safe-exchange spot or a busy public place, and check cards (light test, loupe, cert numbers) before paying.
- Before going live, consider a short custom domain (e.g. a .ca you own) instead of the railway.app link: it looks more trustworthy on posters and in ads.
